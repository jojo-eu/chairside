# Chairside / Katarína Dentist Discovery Outreach Pack

This pack turns the working Katarína demo into a focused customer discovery motion for Slovak and Czech dental clinics.

## Purpose

The goal is to use the current working Katarína voice demo to learn from real clinics before building more product.

This pack is for:

- Turning the working Katarína demo into customer discovery.
- Targeting Slovak and Czech dental clinics.
- Validating reception pain, willingness to try, and pilot interest.
- Avoiding more product work until clinic feedback is concrete.

## ICP

Target clinics:

- Small and medium dental clinics in Slovakia and Czechia.
- Clinics with phone-heavy reception load.
- Clinics that handle appointment booking, confirmations, cancellations, and rescheduling by phone.
- Clinics where missed calls, no-shows, or last-minute cancellations matter financially.
- Clinics with an owner, clinic manager, or head receptionist who can describe the current workflow.

Exclude for now:

- Generic non-dental use cases.
- Broad AI receptionist use cases outside dental clinics.
- Clinics that only want a generic website chatbot.
- Complex enterprise chains unless they can pilot one narrow clinic workflow.

## Demo Asset Summary

Current demo asset:

- Katarína voice receptionist.
- Slovak dental receptionist persona.
- 60-90 second Slovak demo.
- Preventive check-up booking flow.
- Offers three deterministic slots:
  - `streda 10:30`
  - `štvrtok 14:00`
  - `piatok 9:15`
- Confirms the selected slot after patient confirmation.
- No SMS sending in the demo.
- No production scheduling claims.
- No real appointment database write.

## Core Value Proposition

Chairside / Katarína helps dental clinics handle routine reception work without taking staff out of control.

Core value:

- Answers routine calls.
- Books or checks simple appointments.
- Reduces receptionist interruptions.
- Handles reminder responses and conflict review internally.
- Keeps staff in control of exceptions and sensitive workflows.

Short version:

```text
Katarína je hlasová AI recepčná pre zubné ambulancie. Vie vybaviť jednoduché telefonáty, ponúknuť termíny a nechať zložité prípady recepcii.
```

## What To Say Clearly

Say these things plainly:

- This is a prototype/demo.
- We are looking for feedback from dentists and clinic managers.
- This is not a request for a long sales call.
- A pilot would be narrow and supervised.
- The assistant does not provide medical advice.
- Staff stay in control of edge cases.

## What Not To Promise

Do not promise:

- Full production scheduling yet.
- Integration with every dental system.
- Autonomous rescheduling.
- SMS automation without approval.
- Medical advice.
- Insurance advice.
- A generic AI OS.
- Full replacement of reception staff.

## Demo Call Structure

Use a 15-minute discovery call:

1. 2 minutes context:
   - Explain this is a prototype for dental clinics.
   - Set expectation that feedback is more valuable than politeness.
2. 90 seconds demo:
   - Play or run the Katarína booking flow.
3. 8-10 minutes questions:
   - Ask about reception load, missed calls, no-shows, reminders, and scheduling pain.
4. 2 minutes next step:
   - Ask whether a supervised narrow pilot would be worth exploring.
   - Agree on one follow-up action or close politely.

## Discovery Questions

Ask:

- Roughly how many patient calls do you receive per day?
- When is reception most overloaded?
- Do missed calls happen? What usually causes them?
- How much do no-shows or late cancellations hurt the clinic?
- How do you handle reminder confirmations today?
- What happens when a patient replies that they cannot come?
- Which cancellations or rescheduling cases are most painful?
- What software do you currently use for appointments?
- Which appointment types are simple enough for a receptionist to book quickly?
- Would you test a supervised pilot for one narrow workflow?
- What would make a voice assistant unacceptable for your clinic?
- What would staff need to see before trusting it?
- Who would need to approve a pilot?

## Qualification Signals

Strong signals:

- The clinic describes clear phone pain.
- Owner, manager, or head receptionist is engaged.
- Reception is a real bottleneck.
- Missed calls, no-shows, or cancellations have visible cost.
- Appointment categories are simple enough to start narrow.
- They are willing to test with limited scope and human supervision.
- They can name one specific workflow they want help with.

## Red Flags

Watch for:

- No meaningful phone pain.
- Very complex scheduling rules from day one.
- Wants full integration immediately.
- Wants autonomous medical advice automation.
- No access to decision maker.
- Wants only a generic chatbot.
- Wants the assistant to replace staff judgment.
- Is uncomfortable with a supervised prototype.

## Pilot Proposal Draft

Pilot positioning:

```text
Navrhoval by som veľmi úzky pilot, nie veľkú integráciu. Vybrali by sme jeden bezpečný prípad použitia, nastavili jasné hranice a nechali recepciu pod kontrolou.
```

Pilot shape:

- Narrow and supervised.
- One clinic or one location first.
- One use case first:
  - Appointment confirmation / reminder response, or
  - Preventive check-up booking.
- Staff-visible review for uncertain cases.
- No medical advice.
- No SMS automation unless explicitly approved.
- No real appointment writes until guardrails and approval are defined.

Potential success criteria:

- Fewer routine interruptions for reception.
- Faster handling of simple calls or reminder responses.
- Fewer unresolved missed calls.
- Staff agree the assistant stays within safe boundaries.
- Patients understand they are speaking with a receptionist-style assistant.
